### MEMORANDUM OF POINTS AND AUTHORITIES

(*Id.* at 1.) Plaintiff does not ask to strike the Reply; he requests a brief opportunity to answer what is new.

#### II. LEGAL STANDARD

Once a reply is filed, no additional memoranda may be filed without prior Court approval. Civil L.R. 7-3(d); *Dutta v. State Farm Mut. Auto. Ins. Co.*, 895 F.3d 1166, 1171–72 (9th Cir. 2018). A court "need not consider arguments raised for the first time in a reply brief." *Zamani v. Carnes*, 491 F.3d 990, 997 (9th Cir. 2007). Where the Court may consider new reply matter, good cause exists to permit a short sur-reply so the opposing party has a fair opportunity to respond.

#### III. THE REPLY RAISES FIVE NEW MATTERS

1. **Qualified immunity for opening the car door (Count IV).** The opening motion's immunity argument addressed only probable cause to search and arrest. (Dkt. 22 at 12:7–13:21; Dkt. 27 at 17:24–27.) The reply argues for the first time that Defendants are immune for opening the door of a car occupied by an "unconscious" driver. (Dkt. 33 at 4:28–5:3, 8:5–8.)
2. **New authority to bar amendment.** The reply invokes *Airs Aromatics, LLC v. Victoria's Secret Stores Brand Mgmt., Inc.*, 744 F.3d 595, 600 (9th Cir. 2014)—uncited in the opening motion—to argue Plaintiff may not conform FAC ¶ 16 to the recording. (Dkt. 33 at 5:4–12, 9:21–24.)
3. **New basis to dismiss Counts II and VI with prejudice.** The reply argues Plaintiff's limited non-opposition requires denial of leave to amend, citing new authority, *Argueta v. Walgreens Co.*, 760 F. Supp. 3d 1028, 1033 (E.D. Cal. 2024). (Dkt. 33 at 2:1–13, 9:25–10:4.)
4. **Use of a form-label correction as an "odor" concession.** The reply treats Plaintiff's correction of what a CHP form field says as factual support for probable cause and immunity. (Dkt. 33 at 6:7–9, 8:9–13.)
5. **New request for Rule 12(b)(6) dismissal of punitive damages.** For the first time on reply, Defendants ask the Court to treat the punitive-damages prayer "as a separate claim within the scope of" Rule 12(b)(6). (Dkt. 33 at 10:5–12.)

Exhibit A addresses only these five matters. Granting leave causes no prejudice or delay: the hearing is nearly four weeks away, Exhibit A is attached now, and the reply itself was filed late. As stated in the accompanying Declaration under Civil L.R. 7-11(a), Plaintiff requested a stipulation from defense counsel and could not obtain one.

#### IV. CONCLUSION

Plaintiff respectfully requests that the Court grant leave and deem Exhibit A filed, or, alternatively, disregard the arguments and requests raised for the first time in Defendants' untimely Reply (Dkt. 33).
