## Reading guide

- **Dkt. 49, PDF pp. 3–4** describes the pleaded screening and adjudication workflow. Those are allegations summarized by the court.
- **Dkt. 49, PDF pp. 23–26** explains the disposition. The court granted HireRight judgment on the § 1681b(b)(3)(A) claim without leave to amend, denied the Auto Club's motion, and dismissed the second through fourth alternative claims. The order provides a comparison for screening adjudication and pre-adverse notice. It does not resolve the Hertz matter's proposed § 1681e(b) accuracy claim.
