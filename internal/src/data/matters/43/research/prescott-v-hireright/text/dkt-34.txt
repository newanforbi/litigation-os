         3        A.    Plaintiff Has Not Pled Sufficient Facts to Support A Willful Violation
         4           Plaintiff’s “alternate” claim for a willful violation of Section 607(a) assumes
         5    that, if he was the Auto Club’s independent contractor, HireRight Solutions is liable
         6   for failing “to take reasonable efforts to verify that the use [by the Auto Club] was
         7   permissible.”  (Opp. at 6.)  He  is wrong.   Plaintiff’s status as an employee or
         8   independent contractor of the Auto Club when Advanced Towing terminated his
         9   employment in February 2011  is immaterial; either way,  Plaintiff has not pled
        10   sufficient facts to satisfy his burden under Safeco to allege that HireRight Solutions
        11   violated clearly established law by providing to the Auto Club the results of Plaintiff’s
        12   negative adjudication. Safeco, 551 U.S. at 69-70.

        14   established law.  Starting at page 15 of his opposition to the Auto Club’s separate
