On April 7, 2014, at 10:00 am., Defendant HireRight Solutions, Inc.’s
(“Defendant”) Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings came on regularly for hearing
before Courtroom 1600 of the United States District Court for the Central District, the
Honorable Michael W. Fitzgerald presiding. After full consideration of the papers,
evidence and authorities submitted by the parties and in the Court’s files, as well as
the parties’ oral argument thereon, and good cause appearing therefore,

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED THAT:

Defendant’s Motion for Judgment on the Pleadings is GRANTED and, upon|
finding that Defendant is entitled to judgment as matter of law, all causes of action in
Plaintiff's Second Amended Class Action Complaint asserted against Defendant shall
be, and hereby are, dismissed with prejudice and JUDGMENT is hereby entered in
favor of Defendant on all claims asserted by Plaintiff.

IT IS SO ORDERED.

. FITZGE
