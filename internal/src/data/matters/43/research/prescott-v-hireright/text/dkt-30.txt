TO PLAINTIFF FELIX PRESCOTT AND HIS ATTORNEYS OF
RECORD:

PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that on April 7, 2014, or as soon thereafter as the
matter may. be heard, in Courtroom 1600 of the United States District Court for the
Central District of California, located at 312 North Spring Street, Los Angeles, CA
90012, Defendant HireRight Solutions, Inc., (“Defendant”) will, and hereby does,
move this Court for judgment on the pleadings in its favor as to Plaintiff Felix
Prescott’s (“Plaintiff”) causes of action in his Second Amended Class Action
Complaint pursuant to Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 12(c).

Defendant seeks dismissal of Plaintiff's Second Amended Class Action
Complaint on the grounds that Plaintiff fails to sufficiently plead and allege any
violations of the federal Fair Credit Reporting Act, including any willful violations,
and therefore, his claims fail on the pleadings.

This ‘Motion is based upon this Notice of Motion and Motion, the
accompanying Memorandum of Points and Authorities filed in support of this Motion,
the Declaration of Jennifer L. Mora, and all exhibits attached thereto, as well as the
pleadings, records and files in this case, and upon such other, further oral and
documentary evidence that may be presented at or before hearing on this matter.

This Motion is made following an unsuccessful meet and confer effort between
counsel pursuant to L.R. 7-3. (See Declaration of Jennifer L. Mora, {| 7 and 8, filed

and served concurrently herewith.)
