## Verified against official sources
- CCP 416.10 (leginfo): agent under Corp Code 202/1502/2105/2107, OR president/head, VP, secretary, treasurer, controller/CFO, general manager, or authorized person.
- CCP 412.30 (leginfo): if the notice on behalf of the corporation/association isn't on the served summons, no default may be taken.
- CCP 414.10 (leginfo): any non-party aged 18 or older.
- CCP 415.10 (leginfo): personal delivery; complete at the time of delivery.
- CCP 415.20 (leginfo): (a) entities: leave at office with person apparently in charge, then mail; (b) individuals: after reasonable diligence; complete on the 10th day after mailing. Current version is repealed Jan 1, 2027. The new operative version (AB 747, Stats. 2025 ch. 563) requires 3 attempts on 3 days at 3 times and adds Priority/Certified mail options.
- CCP 415.30 (leginfo): two copies of the notice and acknowledgment plus a prepaid return envelope; complete on the date the acknowledgment is signed, if returned; 20 days; expense shifting.
- CCP 412.20(a)(3): 30 days to respond. CCP 583.210/583.250: 3-year limit; mandatory dismissal.
- Corp Code 1505 (leginfo): corporate agent certificate naming the employees authorized to accept.
- CRC 3.110(b) (courts.ca.gov): 60 days to serve and file proofs; 30 days for added defendants. 3.110(e): extension application must be filed before the deadline.
- CRC 3.55(5): sheriff/marshal fees waived under an initial fee waiver. CRC 3.221(c): ADR package must be served with the complaint.
- FRCP 4(c)(2), 4(c)(3), 4(d), 4(e), 4(h), 4(m) (law.cornell.edu). 28 USC 1448 (Cornell).

- POS-010 (Rev. Jan 1, 2007): items 2 (documents), 3a/3b (party/person), 5 (method), 6 (capacity). POS-015: sender must not be a party.
- Bizfile search is at bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov (bizfile.sos.ca.gov redirects to the SOS bizfile info page).

## Corrections / softenings of source claims
1. Lesson 4, SER-001 3b(2)/3b(3): the item numbers match the current form, but the form itself does NOT say to leave 3b(2) blank. The article frames this as one sheriff's office's practice and a safe default, not a form rule.
2. Lesson 7, "person of suitable age": that is the federal Rule 4(e)(2)(B) phrase. California's 415.20 requires a person at least 18 who is apparently in charge (or a competent household member) and is told what the papers are. The article uses the California standard.
3. Lesson 2/5, "manager" not valid: 416.10(b) DOES allow service on a "general manager." The article doesn't say serving a manager is always invalid. It says the shared-lot service didn't serve the other corporation, and that a proof marked "individual" doesn't show service on the corporation.
4. Lesson 6, substitute service: the article clarifies that 415.20(a) (entities) and 415.20(b) (individuals, after diligence) are different. It flags the 2027 three-attempt diligence rule. The source's "two attempts" is reported as fact without claiming it was sufficient.
5. Lesson 9, removal: the article adds 28 USC 1448 for context and keeps the outcome limited to what the source says.
6. Sheriff fees are mentioned only generically; no dollar figure. The $134 is the private server invoice from the source. The flat fee quote is mentioned without an amount (none in the source).
