## Glossary of terms used in this case

- **Superior Court:** the general trial court in each of California's 58 counties.
- **Unlimited civil:** a California civil case seeking more than $35,000; "limited civil" cases below that amount follow simpler procedures.
- **Civil case cover sheet:** a standard form that helps the court classify a new filing.
- **Fee waiver:** the state-court counterpart to federal in forma pauperis status, excusing court fees for people who qualify financially.
- **Summons:** notice to each defendant of the lawsuit and the deadline to respond.
- **Proof of service / personal service:** a sworn form confirming delivery, and delivery made by hand to the person or an authorized recipient.
- **Case management conference:** a hearing with the judge about status and scheduling.
- **Demurrer:** California's equivalent of a motion to dismiss; it asks whether the complaint, accepting its factual allegations, states a legally valid claim. A judge who agrees *sustains* it; one who disagrees *overrules* it.
- **Reply:** the moving side's final brief, answering the opposition.
- **Tentative ruling:** a preliminary indication, issued before a hearing, of how the judge expects to rule.
- **Oral argument:** the chance to address the judge in person before a tentative ruling becomes final.
- **Minute order:** the court's official written account of what occurred at a hearing.
- **Pro se / pro per / in propria persona:** representing oneself.
- **Exhaustion of administrative remedies:** a rule that, in some disputes, an available agency or administrative process must be completed before going to court.
- **Jurisdictional:** treated as a limit on the court's power to hear the case.
- **Without leave to amend:** no further amended complaint allowed.
- **Vacated:** removed from the calendar.
- **Tort:** a civil wrong, apart from breach of contract, for which damages may be awarded.
- **d/b/a:** "doing business as."

## Step-by-step: how the case has moved

**1. Opening the case (February 10, 2026).** Newanforbi filed an unlimited civil complaint with a summons and cover sheet. The court granted a fee waiver and calendared a case management conference for October 13, 2026.

California fee waivers fall under Government Code § 68630 and the sections that follow, and they are requested on Judicial Council form FW-001. An approved waiver can cover filing fees and some other court costs.

On the pleading side, California is often called a "fact pleading" state: Code of Civil Procedure § 425.10 asks for a statement of the facts making up the cause of action, in ordinary and concise language. The emphasis differs from the federal plausibility test, though both ask whether enough has been alleged to state a claim.

**2. Serving the defendants (February 24 and March 26, 2026).** Proofs of service on file show personal service. A California summons ordinarily allows 30 days after service for a response, and personal service has to be carried out by an adult who is not a party.

The defendants listed are Katie Palacios, Clinical Director; HOPE Program (Stockton California); and HOPE Psycotherapy, Inc., d/b/a HOPE Program.

**3. The demurrer briefing (through July 7, 2026).** The defendants demurred to the First Amended Complaint and filed their reply on July 7. Code of Civil Procedure § 430.10 lists the grounds for a demurrer, which is confined to the face of the complaint and matters subject to judicial notice. Section 430.41 generally requires the demurring side to meet and confer with the plaintiff first. When a demurrer is sustained, leave to amend is usually granted if there is a reasonable possibility of curing the defect, and the plaintiff carries the burden of showing how.

**4. The tentative ruling (July 10, 2026).** UniCourt shows a tentative ruling published on this date. California Rule of Court 3.1308 allows superior courts to adopt tentative-ruling systems. The San Joaquin court's website describes its procedure for civil law-and-motion matters in Stockton: tentatives are posted electronically by 1:30 p.m. on the day before the hearing, and anyone wishing to argue must notify the court and the other side by 4:00 p.m. that day; otherwise the tentative becomes the ruling without argument.

**5. The hearing and ruling (July 15, 2026).** According to the [July 15, 2026 minute order on the demurrer, posted on the Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/brendan-ngehsi-newanforbi-v-palacios-minute-order-demurrer-san-joaquin-stk-cv-uf), Newanforbi appeared pro se and requested oral argument. The court affirmed its tentative ruling, agreed with the defendants that exhaustion of administrative remedies was a jurisdictional requirement, found that Newanforbi had not satisfied it before filing the First Amended Complaint, and sustained the demurrer without leave to amend. A formal order was signed that day.

**6. Status afterward.** UniCourt lists the case as closed or disposed and marks the October 13, 2026 case management conference as vacated.

## How to look up the file

California trial court records are generally open unless sealed or confidential by law. UniCourt is a private service that compiles and organizes docket data; Trellis is another research service indexing California trial court records; and the Internet Archive is a nonprofit digital library.

The official source is the court itself. Its website, sjcourts.org, provides a Case Management Search through the eCourt Public Portal, searchable by case number or party name. California Rule of Court 2.507 limits online party searches to names, so typing in the full case number, STK-CV-UF-2026-0001032, is the surest route. A name search may require the caption's spelling, "Brenden Nghesi Newanforbi." The court cautions that its portal is not the official record; official copies can be requested from the clerk's office. State dockets do not carry federal ECF numbers. Entries are identified by date and title, and minute orders by hearing date.

## Self-representation in California courts

Californians may represent themselves in civil matters, and the courts variously use "self-represented," "pro per," and "in propria persona." Self-represented parties generally must follow the same procedural and evidentiary rules as lawyers. The minute order records that Newanforbi appeared pro se at the July 15, 2026 hearing. Every superior court has a self-help center providing free information and help with forms, though the case types each covers vary. The Judicial Council's online California Courts Self-Help Guide explains many civil procedures, and county law libraries, bar lawyer referral services, legal aid groups, and limited-scope attorneys are other options.
