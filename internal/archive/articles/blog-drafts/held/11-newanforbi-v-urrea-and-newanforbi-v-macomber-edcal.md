# Newanforbi v. Urrea and Newanforbi v. Macomber: Case Overview and Court Record (E.D. Cal. 2:26-cv-00190 and 2:26-cv-00699)

Brendan Ngehsi Newanforbi filed two short-lived civil rights cases in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of California in early 2026. Both list Jeff Macomber as a defendant, and Newanforbi voluntarily dismissed both. This post covers them together.

## Newanforbi v. Urrea (2:26-cv-00190)

According to the [Newanforbi v. Urrea docket on PacerMonitor](https://www.pacermonitor.com/public/case/62735010/PS_Newanforbi_v_Urrea_et_al):

- **Filed:** January 22, 2026
- **Judges:** Dena M. Coggins; referred to Magistrate Judge Sean C. Riordan
- **Nature of suit:** 440 Civil Rights – Other; 42 U.S.C. § 1983
- **Defendants:** Urrea, G. Noguchi, Jeff Macomber

Docket events:

- **January 22, 2026:** Newanforbi filed the complaint, a motion to proceed in forma pauperis, and a motion to e-file.
- **January 28, 2026:** He filed a First Amended Complaint against all defendants.
- **June 29, 2026:** He filed a notice of voluntary dismissal under FRCP 41(a)(1)(A)(ii).
- **July 2, 2026:** A minute order stated that, "pursuant to the Plaintiff's Notice of Voluntary Dismissal," the action was dismissed with prejudice, and the case was closed.

## Newanforbi v. Macomber (2:26-cv-00699)

- **Judges:** Presiding Judge Dena M. Coggins; Referral Judge Jeremy D. Peterson
- **Case type:** Civil – Constitutional and Civil Rights

Docket events:

- **June 30, 2026:** The case was closed under that notice.
