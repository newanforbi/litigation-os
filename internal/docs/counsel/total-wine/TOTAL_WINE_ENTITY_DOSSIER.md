## 1. Executive summary: top 10 findings

1. **Your employer of record is California Fine Wine & Spirits, LLC d/b/a Total Wine & More**, main office 6600 Rockledge Drive, Suite 150, Bethesda, MD 20817, phone 301-795-1000. The Labor Code § 2810.5 notice and the arbitration agreement both say so. [DOC]
2. **The background report is going to the Bethesda headquarters under the brand name, not to the California LLC.** First Advantage's notice says it is "reporting public record information to Total Wine & More at 6600 Rockledge Dr Ste 150, Bethesda." That is the address of Retail Services & Systems, Inc. (RSSI), which a Maryland appellate court describes as the company that "provides administrative services to retail stores operating under the 'Total Wine' brand name." [DOC] + [SOURCED: Kasmir](https://www.mdcourts.gov/data/opinions/cosa/2025/1778s23.pdf)
3. **The arbitration agreement is a condition of hire, uses JAMS, delegates arbitrability to the arbitrator, and waives class, collective, and representative actions.** It also contains two clauses that are attack points under California unconscionability law (¶ 17 fee-shifting for filing in court; ¶ 2 waiver of monetary recovery from agency charges). [DOC] Details in Part 6.
4. **I found no public ruling on a Total Wine motion to compel arbitration against an employee, in California or elsewhere.** That is a gap in what I could reach, not proof that none exists. [UNVERIFIED]
5. **Active California wage-and-hour class/PAGA litigation is pending in S.D. Cal.**: *Potter v. California Fine Wine & Spirits LLC*, No. 3:25-cv-03286-AJB-MSB, consolidated with two related cases on or about 2026-05-04. The company removed each case from state court and opposed remand. [SOURCED: Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/daily-labor-report/case-wage-hour-california-class-actions-s-d-cal-6), [Justia docket](https://dockets.justia.com/docket/california/casdce/3:2025cv03286/835789)
6. **A FEHA disability/harassment/constructive-discharge case is pending in your district**: *Saucedo v. California Fine Wine & Spirits LLC*, N.D. Cal. No. 3:26-cv-00318, originally filed 2025-12-08 in Contra Costa Superior Court. The court issued a published-to-govinfo order or opinion dated about 2026-10-20. This is the single most useful docket to pull. [SOURCED: govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/USCOURTS-cand-3_26-cv-00318), [Law.com Radar](https://www.law.com/radar/card/ca-contracostacounty-655881-saucedo-v-california-fine-wine-spirits-llc/)
7. **Current California defense counsel is Jackson Lewis P.C., Silicon Valley office (San Jose)**: Karin Cogbill, Sean Bothamley, Andres Antuna, in *Potter* and *Nguyen*. [SOURCED: Justia docket](https://dockets.justia.com/docket/california/casdce/3:2025cv03286/835789), [Jackson Lewis bio](https://www.jacksonlewis.com/people/sean-bothamley), [State Bar](https://apps.calbar.ca.gov/attorney/Licensee/Detail/244606)
8. **One prior California class settlement is public**: *Blackshear v. California Fine Wine & Spirits LLC*, Sacramento Superior Court No. 34-2018-00245842, class period 2014-12-05 to 2020-02-15, final approval hearing 2020-12-01. A plaintiff-firm declaration filed in another case describes it as a $1,800,000 settlement. [SOURCED for case and dates: settlement site](https://cawineclassactionsettlement.com/); [SINGLE for amount](https://www.cptgroupcaseinfo.com/WestCovinaCorporateFitnessSettlement/Declaration%20of%20Kyle%20Nordrehaug.pdf)
9. **No EEOC lawsuit, EEOC consent decree, or California CRD enforcement action against any Total Wine entity turned up.** The public government record is mostly alcohol-pricing and antitrust: FTC subpoena enforcement (2023), Connecticut and Massachusetts pricing penalties (2016–2017), a Seattle hazard-pay settlement (2021, $333,019.57), two OSHA penalties (2021), and Prop 65 notices. [SOURCED, see Part 4]
10. **The company is large, private, and family-held, with capacity to pay any individual employment judgment.** About $6.39 billion in 2025 U.S. retail sales per the NRF Top 100 as reported by a trade blog [SINGLE], about $6 billion in 2023 per Forbes as cited by Wikipedia [SOURCED](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_Wine_%26_More); roughly 280 to 305 stores in about 30 states, 46 to 49 in California. New CEO Ryan Ross started 2026-01-12. [SOURCED: PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/total-wine--more-welcomes-ryan-ross-as-chief-executive-officer-302649097.html)
