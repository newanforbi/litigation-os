**Why this is the strongest state formulation:**
- It leads with the only claim provable from documents already in hand.
- It uses Doe relation-back, instead of naming brands without facts, to preserve Howell, Kanwell, Marriott, and HHG.
- It keeps § 290.46 out until a source fact exists, so the complaint does not invite a *Lerma*-style demurrer.
- It avoids a removal hook.
- It pleads attendance facts candidly, which blunts the *Donaleski* defense's surprise value.
